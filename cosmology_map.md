## Introduction

This document presents a **disciplined map** of modern cosmology — from the Big Bang to planetary habitability — audited using the Delta principles: H₃ Warrant (falsifiability), Friction Score (interpretive divergence), MCL Coefficient (causal confidence), and Boundary Statement (epistemic humility).

The map does not claim certainty. It reports confidence, divergence, falsifiability conditions, and boundaries. It is a tool for disciplined truth-seeking, not a dogmatic statement.

**Unit of measurement:** Dys (D) — one unit of disciplined agreement.

### What is the Dys?

The **dys** (symbol: D) is the unit of **disciplined agreement**. It measures how much confidence can be placed in a claim, a score, or an assessment — based on transparency, reproducibility, and consensus.

Unlike physical units (meter, second, kilogram), the dys does not measure a property of the universe. It measures a property of **human understanding** — specifically, the disciplined process of testing, debating, and agreeing on claims.

| Dys Value | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| 0.90 – 1.00 D | Exceptional confidence. Widely agreed, highly falsifiable, strong causal evidence. |
| 0.70 – 0.89 D | High confidence. Well-established, some divergence or minor gaps. |
| 0.50 – 0.69 D | Moderate confidence. Plausible, but significant uncertainty or disagreement. |
| 0.30 – 0.49 D | Low confidence. Weak evidence, high divergence, or poor falsifiability. |
| 0.00 – 0.29 D | Very low confidence. Speculative, contested, or unfalsifiable. |

### How the Dys Is Used in This Document

Each claim in this map is accompanied by four Delta metrics:

| Metric | Symbol | What It Measures |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **H₃ Warrant** | (implicit) | What would prove this claim wrong (falsifiability) |
| **Friction Score** | D | How much disagreement exists among experts (0.00–1.00 D) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | D | How confident we are in the causal link (0.00–1.00 D) |
| **Boundary Statement** | (text) | What this claim does not address — known unknowns |

The **Integrity Score** (when calculated) is a weighted combination of these four metrics. Higher scores indicate greater disciplined agreement.

All scores are reported with their boundaries. No claim is presented as certain. The map is a tool for disciplined truth-seeking, not a dogmatic statement.

| **H₃ Warrant** | Evidence that the universe is not expanding; or that the cosmic microwave background is not a relic of a hot early phase; or a validated alternative steady-state model. |

## Coordinate 2: Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB)

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | The universe is filled with faint microwave radiation — a near-perfect blackbody spectrum at 2.725 K — which is the cooled remnant of the hot, dense plasma from the early universe, released when atoms first formed (recombination) ~380,000 years after the Big Bang. |
| **H₃ Warrant** | If the CMB spectrum significantly deviates from blackbody; or if its anisotropies do not match structure formation; or if an alternative non-primordial source is proven. |
| **Friction Score** | 0.05 D (Very Low — near-universal consensus) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.98 D (Extremely High — predicted, discovered, confirmed by multiple satellites) |
| **Boundary Statement** | We do not claim to know the exact physics of recombination at every point; we do not claim that the CMB reveals the universe's state before recombination (opaque era). |

## Coordinate 3: Cosmic Inflation

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | In the first tiny fraction of a second after the Big Bang (from roughly 10⁻³⁶ to 10⁻³² seconds), the universe underwent a brief period of exponential expansion, increasing in size by a factor of at least 10²⁶, smoothing out any initial irregularities and explaining the observed flatness, homogeneity, and isotropy of the cosmos. |
| **H₃ Warrant** | If the predicted primordial gravitational wave signature (B-mode polarization in the CMB) is definitively ruled out; or if an alternative mechanism (e.g., bouncing cosmology) explains the observed flatness and homogeneity without inflation. |
| **Friction Score** | 0.40 D (Moderate — leading paradigm but significant alternative models exist) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.75 D (High — successful predictions, but lack of unique model tempers confidence) |
| **Boundary Statement** | We do not claim to know what caused inflation, what the inflaton field is (if it exists), whether inflation is eternal, or whether it produces a multiverse. |

## Coordinate 5: Dark Matter

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | A non-luminous, non-baryonic form of matter that does not interact with light but exerts gravity. It comprises approximately 85% of all matter in the universe and is essential to explain galaxy rotation curves, gravitational lensing, galaxy cluster dynamics, and large-scale structure formation. |
| **H₃ Warrant** | If a modified theory of gravity (e.g., MOND, emergent gravity) explains all dark matter phenomena without new particles; or if a specific particle (WIMP, axion) is detected; or if ΛCDM fails a critical prediction. |
| **Friction Score** | 0.45 D (Moderate — consensus paradigm but significant friction from modified gravity advocates and lack of particle detection) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.80 D (High — evidence for an anomaly is overwhelming; identity of the anomaly is uncertain) |
| **Boundary Statement** | We do not claim to know what dark matter is (particle, field, or modified gravity). We do not claim that ΛCDM is the final word. We claim that a gravitational anomaly exists and is robustly observed. |

## Coordinate 6: Dark Energy

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|

| **H₃ Warrant** | If Type Ia supernova distance measurements are systematically flawed; or if a modified gravity theory explains acceleration without dark energy; or if future surveys find no evidence of cosmic acceleration. |

| **Boundary Statement** | We do not claim to know what dark energy is (cosmological constant, quintessence, modified gravity). We do not claim to know whether dark energy will remain constant, decay, or reverse. |

|-----------|------------|

| **H₃ Warrant** | If a massive star disappears without a supernova; if neutrinos are not detected from a nearby supernova. |
