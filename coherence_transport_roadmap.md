\## The Hypothesis



| Concept | Symbol | Definition |

|---------|--------|------------|

| Coherence | Cf | The degree to which an object's motion aligns with the ambient gravity gradient (0 = random, 1 = perfect) |

| Amplification | A | A = 1 + α·ρ\_norm + β·Cf + γ·ρ\_norm·Cf |

| Emergent gravity | F\_eff = G·m1·m2/r² × A | Gravity is not generated by mass; it is concentrated from a pre-existing field |



\*\*Core claim:\*\* If Cf → 1, an object's effective inertial mass approaches zero, allowing travel times comparable to information (light speed).

\## The Roadmap



| Phase | Milestone | Timeframe | Success Metric |

|-------|-----------|-----------|----------------|

| \*\*1\*\* | Prove Cf is measurable | 5–10 years | Peer-reviewed detection of Cf in rotating mass experiment |

| \*\*2\*\* | Build Cf sensor | 10–15 years | Portable device (1 kg, 10 W) can measure Cf at 1% precision |

| \*\*5\*\* | Detect inertial mass reduction | 40–60 years | Effective mass reduced by >10% at Cf > 0.9 |

| \*\*7\*\* | Human-scale coherence | 80–120 years | Human subject achieves Cf > 0.9, survives |

| \*\*8\*\* | Interplanetary travel | 100–150 years | Earth → Mars in minutes (not months) |

| \*\*9\*\* | Interstellar travel | 150–200 years | Earth → Proxima Centauri in hours (not millennia) |

\## Falsification



This roadmap is falsifiable. If:



| Falsification | By | Then |

|---------------|-----|------|

| No Cf detection after 20 years | Lack of experimental progress | Hypothesis is not testable or Cf is negligible |

| Cf cannot be controlled after 40 years | Engineering limits | Coherence is not a tunable parameter |

| No inertial mass reduction after 60 years | Negative experimental results | Coherence does not affect mass |

| No coherent "jump" after 100 years | Repeated failures | The hypothesis is likely false |

\## The Boundary Statement



This roadmap assumes the emergent gravity hypothesis is correct. If the hypothesis is false, the roadmap is irrelevant.



We do not claim that coherence transport will happen. We claim that \*\*if it is possible, this is the most likely timeline\*\*.



The purpose of this document is not to predict the future. It is to provide a \*\*falsifiable framework\*\* — so that progress (or lack of progress) can be measured against explicit milestones.
