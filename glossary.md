## Delta Principles

### H₃ Warrant
What would prove a claim wrong. The test of falsifiability. A claim without an H₃ Warrant is not yet disciplined.

### Friction Score (D)
How much experts disagree about a claim. Low friction (0.00–0.30 D) = consensus. High friction (0.70–1.00 D) = contested.

### MCL Coefficient (D)
How confident we are that a cause leads to an effect. Measures causal strength. High MCL (0.90–1.00 D) = strong evidence. Low MCL = correlation only.

### Boundary Statement
What a claim does not address. Epistemic humility — stating known unknowns.

### Integrity Score (D)
Weighted combination of H₃, Friction, MCL, and Boundary. A single number representing disciplined agreement.

## Scientific Terms (as used in these publications)

### CMB (Cosmic Microwave Background)
Afterglow of the Big Bang. Temperature 2.725 K. One of the strongest pieces of evidence for the hot early universe.

### Inflation
Brief period of exponential expansion in the first 10⁻³² seconds after the Big Bang. Explains flatness and homogeneity.

### Dark Matter
Invisible mass (85% of all matter) inferred from galaxy rotation curves and gravitational lensing. Particle unknown.

### Dark Energy
Unknown force accelerating cosmic expansion. 68% of universe's energy budget. Nature unknown.

### Isostasy
Deep root structure of mountains. Confirmed by seismology. Quran 78:6-7 describes mountains as "pegs."

### Kilonova
Explosion from neutron star merger. Produces heavy elements: gold, platinum, uranium. First observed in 2017 (GW170817).

## Balance Law Terms

### Gravity vs. Pressure
In stars: gravity pulls in, fusion pushes out. Balance = star lives. Imbalance = supernova or black hole.

### Diastole vs. Systole
In heart: diastole fills, systole empties. Balance = heartbeat. Imbalance = cardiac arrest.

### Dust vs. Water
In humans: dust (earth, minerals) + water (life, blood). Balance = life. Imbalance = death.

### Inhale vs. Exhale
In breath: inhale brings in, exhale pushes out. Balance = respiration. Imbalance = suffocation.

## Scriptural Terms

### Taurat
Torah — revelation given to Musa (Moses). Core: Shema (Deuteronomy 6:4) — pure monotheism. Later additions: firmament, anthropomorphism.

### Injeel
Gospel — revelation given to Isa (Jesus). Core: parables, ethical teachings. Later additions: Trinity, divinity of Jesus, Pauline theology.

### Zabur
Psalms — revelation given to Dawud (David). Poetic praise. Contains geocentrism (Psalm 104:5).

### Quran
Final revelation — preserved criterion (furqan). Confirms previous, corrects distortions, supplements lost knowledge.

## Historical Figures

### Isaac Newton (1643–1727)
Physicist, mathematician, theologian. Discovered laws of motion and gravity. Wrote more on theology than on science. Rejected Trinity, believed original Christianity was pure monotheism. Hid his manuscripts for fear of prosecution.

### Athanasius (c. 296–373)
Bishop of Alexandria. Primary defender of Trinitarianism at Council of Nicaea (325 AD). Newton blamed him for corrupting original Christianity.

### Arius (c. 250–336)
Christian presbyter from Alexandria. Taught that Jesus was a created being, not co-eternal with God. Newton was Arian in his theology.
