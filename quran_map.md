## What is the Dys?

The **dys** (symbol: D) is the unit of **disciplined agreement**. It measures how much confidence can be placed in a claim, a score, or an assessment — based on transparency, reproducibility, and consensus.

Unlike physical units (meter, second, kilogram), the dys does not measure a property of the universe. It measures a property of **human understanding** — specifically, the disciplined process of testing, debating, and agreeing on claims.

| Dys Value | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| 0.90 – 1.00 D | Exceptional confidence. Widely agreed, highly falsifiable, strong causal evidence. |
| 0.70 – 0.89 D | High confidence. Well-established, some divergence or minor gaps. |
| 0.50 – 0.69 D | Moderate confidence. Plausible, but significant uncertainty or disagreement. |
| 0.30 – 0.49 D | Low confidence. Weak evidence, high divergence, or poor falsifiability. |
| 0.00 – 0.29 D | Very low confidence. Speculative, contested, or unfalsifiable. |

### How the Dys Is Used in This Document

Each claim in this map is accompanied by four Delta metrics:

| Metric | Symbol | What It Measures |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **H₃ Warrant** | (implicit) | What would prove this claim wrong (falsifiability) |
| **Friction Score** | D | How much disagreement exists among experts (0.00–1.00 D) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | D | How confident we are in the causal link (0.00–1.00 D) |
| **Boundary Statement** | (text) | What this claim does not address — known unknowns |

All scores are reported with their boundaries. No claim is presented as certain. The map is a tool for disciplined truth-seeking, not a dogmatic statement.

## Coordinate 1: The Origin of the Universe (Quran 21:30)

> *"Do not the disbelievers see that the heavens and the earth were a joined entity, then We separated them, and We made from water every living thing? Then will they not believe?"*

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | The heavens and earth were initially a single joined entity (ratq), then were separated (fatq). |
| **Scientific Referent** | The Big Bang — the universe began from a singularity (single point), then expanded and separated. |
| **H₃ Warrant** | If the universe were found to be static and eternal (no beginning, no expansion); or if the cosmic microwave background did not exist. |
| **Friction Score** | 0.40 D — Moderate. Many Muslim scholars see alignment with the Big Bang; some exegetes interpret "heavens and earth" as the sky and ground (not cosmos). |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.50 D — Moderate. The verse is compatible with the Big Bang but is not a prediction. The causal link is plausible but not necessary. |
| **Boundary Statement** | We do not claim that the verse predicts the Big Bang; we claim it is compatible. We do not claim that the original audience would have understood it as modern cosmology. |

## Conclusion

The Quran contains verses that align with modern science with varying degrees of friction and confidence. Some align closely (orbits, sun/moon light distinction, iron from space, mountains as pegs). Others are compatible but not predictive (Big Bang, expansion). Some describe eschatological or metaphysical realities not subject to empirical falsification (Big Crunch, stars as missiles, prostration). The map does not force harmony. It reports divergence, states boundaries, and calibrates confidence.

The dys (D) is the unit of disciplined agreement. It does not measure physical properties. It measures the quality of human understanding — the transparency, reproducibility, and consensus behind each claim.
