## What Is This Document?

This document presents a **disciplined comparison** of claims from Taurat (Torah), Injeel (Gospel), Zabur (Psalms), Buddha (Dharma), and the Quran — using the Delta principles: H₃ Warrant (falsifiability), Friction Score (interpretive divergence), MCL Coefficient (causal confidence), and Boundary Statement (epistemic humility).

The purpose is not to declare any tradition "false." The purpose is to **map** where claims align, where they diverge, and where they belong to different domains (empirical, metaphorical, metaphysical, eschatological).

---

## What Is the Dys?

The **dys** (symbol: D) is the unit of **disciplined agreement**. It measures how much confidence can be placed in a claim based on transparency, reproducibility, and consensus.

| Dys Value | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| 0.90–1.00 D | Exceptional confidence |
| 0.70–0.89 D | High confidence |
| 0.50–0.69 D | Moderate confidence |
| 0.30–0.49 D | Low confidence |
| 0.00–0.29 D | Very low confidence |

## Map 1: Taurat (Torah) — Genesis Creation

### Genesis 1:1 — Origin of the Universe

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | "In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth." |
| **Scientific Referent** | The Big Bang — finite beginning of the universe. |
| **Friction Score** | 0.40 D (Moderate) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.50 D (Moderate) |
| **Comparison to Quran** | Compatible. Quran 21:30 describes a joined entity separated (Big Bang). |

### Genesis 1:6-8 — The Firmament

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | A solid dome (firmament) separates upper waters from lower waters. |
| **Scientific Referent** | No scientific parallel. Pre-scientific cosmology. |
| **Friction Score** | 0.90 D (Very High) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.10 D (Very Low) |
| **Comparison to Quran** | No such concept. Quran corrects or omits. |

### Deuteronomy 6:4 — The Shema

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | "Hear, O Israel: The Lord our God, the Lord is One." |
| **Theological Referent** | Pure monotheism (tawhid). |
| **Friction Score** | 0.10 D (Low) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.90 D (Very High) |
| **Comparison to Quran** | Strong alignment. Quran 112:1-4 affirms absolute oneness. |

## Map 2: Injeel (Gospel) — Sayings of Jesus

### Matthew 13:31-32 — The Mustard Seed

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | Mustard seed is smallest of seeds, grows into a large plant. |
| **Scientific Referent** | Generally accurate; mustard seeds are among the smallest. |
| **Friction Score** | 0.30 D (Low to Moderate) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.80 D (High) |

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | Jesus shares divine nature with the Father. |
| **Theological Referent** | Trinitarian / Incarnational theology. |
| **Friction Score** | 0.70 D (High) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.40 D (Low to Moderate) |
| **Comparison to Quran** | Quran rejects Trinity and divinity of Jesus (5:72, 5:116). Fundamental divergence. |

## Map 4: Buddha — Dharma

### Samyutta Nikaya 56:11 — The First Noble Truth (Dukkha)

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | Life involves suffering (dukkha). |
| **Scientific Referent** | Psychological — pain, loss, aging are universal. |
| **Friction Score** | 0.20 D (Low) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.75 D (High) |
| **Comparison to Quran** | Compatible as observation of human condition. Quran also acknowledges trials and suffering. |

### Samyutta Nikaya 12:61 — Dependent Origination (Causality)

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Claim** | "When this exists, that comes to be. When this does not exist, that does not come to be." |
| **Scientific Referent** | Causality — fundamental principle of science. |
| **Friction Score** | 0.15 D (Low) |
| **MCL Coefficient** | 0.85 D (High) |
| **Comparison to Quran** | Compatible. Quran repeatedly calls humans to reflect on cause and effect as signs (ayah). |
