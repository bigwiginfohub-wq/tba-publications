\# IBM Quantum Access Guide (Free)



\## Step 1: Sign Up

\- Go to \[quantum.cloud.ibm.com](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com)

\- Create a free IBMid



\## Step 2: Select Open Plan

\- Choose the \*\*Open Plan\*\* (free)

\- You get \*\*10 minutes of runtime every 28 days\*\*



\## Step 3: Use 20 Minutes of Runtime

\- Run small circuits, test Qiskit

\- After reaching 20 minutes, you can \*\*opt in\*\* to a one-time promotion



\## Step 4: Get 180 Free Minutes

\- IBM announced this promotion in March 2026

\- If you use 20 minutes within any 12-month period, you get \*\*180 minutes free for the next 12 months\*\*



\## Step 5: Access the Heron r2 Processor

\- Backend name: `ibm\_kingston`

\- 156 qubits, 340k CLOPS

\- Median two-qubit error rate: `2.03×10⁻³`



\## Step 6: Run the Cf Simulation

\- `python quantum\_cf\_simulation.py`



\## Cost

\- Free tier: 10 minutes/28 days

\- Promotion: 180 minutes free after using 20 minutes

\- Pay-As-You-Go: $1.60/second if you exceed free tier (not needed)
