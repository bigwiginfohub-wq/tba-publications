\## Abstract



A 7th-century text describes a creature, \*Al-Buraq\*, capable of terrestrial and celestial travel at extraordinary speeds, with features that map to known and hypothesized propulsion systems. This paper analyzes that description through the lens of \*\*emergent gravity\*\*, \*\*coherent freefall\*\*, and \*\*gravity gradient amplification\*\*. The analysis yields a novel propulsion architecture: a vehicle that uses minimal initial thrust to achieve coherence with the ambient gravitational field, then amplifies that field via deployable structures ("wings") to achieve high-speed transport without continuous fuel expenditure. Testable predictions and engineering specifications are provided.



\---



\## 1. The Historical Description



Primary sources (Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 3887) describe Al-Buraq as:



\- A white creature, smaller than a mule but larger than a donkey

\- Capable of placing its hooves "at the farthest boundary of one's gaze"

\- Possessing \*\*two wings on its thighs\*\* that "lent strength to its legs"

\- Transporting a passenger from Mecca to Jerusalem (≈1,200 km) in "a part of the night"

\- Then ascending through multiple celestial levels (the "seven heavens")



The name derives from \*barq\* (Arabic for lightning), indicating speed, light emission, or electrical/plasma phenomena.

\*\*Proposed function:\*\* The wings are deployable structures that increase the vehicle's cross-section with the ambient gravitational field, allowing it to \*\*amplify\*\* the field's effect on its motion.



\### 2.3 Coherent Freefall



The Buraq's movement is described as \*smooth\*, \*continuous\*, and \*without vibration\* — consistent with \*\*coherent freefall\*\*. The vehicle is not fighting gravity; it is \*\*falling with it\*\*, but with sufficient tangential velocity to avoid impact.

\## 3. Engineering Model



\### 3.1 Core Equation



From our emergent gravity framework:





F\_eff = (G \* m1 \* m2 / r²) \* (1 + α \* ρ\_norm + β \* Cf + γ \* ρ\_norm \* Cf)





For a vehicle in freefall:



\- \*\*F\_eff\*\* = effective gravitational force on the vehicle

\- \*\*Cf\*\* = coherence factor (how synchronized the vehicle's motion is with the ambient gravity field)

\- \*\*ρ\_norm\*\* = normalized local density (mass distribution around the vehicle)

\- \*\*α, β, γ\*\* = coupling coefficients



\*\*Key insight:\*\* When Cf → 1 (perfect coherence), the effective gravitational force is amplified by (1 + β + γ\*ρ\_norm). The vehicle "falls faster" without additional propulsion.
