\## Proposed Symmetrization (Interim)



To preserve momentum conservation, the amplification factor should apply symmetrically:



F\_ij ∝ A\_i × A\_j





where A\_i and A\_j are the amplification factors of the two interacting bodies.



This ensures action-reaction symmetry.
