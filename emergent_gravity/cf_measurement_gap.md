\## Abstract



Current flight dynamics models (aerodynamics, thermal soaring, dynamic soaring) assume that lift and glide efficiency are fully explained by atmospheric and mechanical forces. This document identifies a missing variable: \*\*coherence (Cf)\*\* — the degree to which a body aligns its motion with the ambient gravity gradient. No existing instrument measures Cf. No existing flight model includes it. This gap may explain anomalies in bird flight (extreme efficiency, night soaring, transoceanic migration) that current models attribute to unmeasured atmospheric conditions.



\---



\## 1. The Missing Variable



| Variable | Symbol | What it measures | Measured in current flight dynamics? |

|----------|--------|------------------|--------------------------------------|

| Coherence | Cf | Alignment of motion with ambient gravity gradient | No |

| Gravity gradient | ∂g/∂r | Change in gravitational field over distance | Yes (satellite gravimetry) |

| Thermal lift | L\_th | Rising hot air | Yes (weather balloons, models) |

| Dynamic soaring | L\_ds | Wind shear energy extraction | Yes (theoretical models) |



\*\*The gap:\*\* Cf is assumed to be 0 (random) or 1 (perfect) but never measured. If Cf varies continuously, then flight efficiency is not solely a function of aerodynamics.



\---



\## 2. Why Cf Is Not Measured



| Reason | Explanation |

|--------|-------------|

| No instrument exists | No sensor measures "coherence with gravity gradient" |

| No theoretical need | Aerodynamics + thermals explain most observations |

| No funding priority | Ornithology and aerospace engineering focus on measurable variables |

| Assumption of isotropy | Gravity is assumed to be uniform and passive, not something to "align with" |



Cf is not measured because no one has asked the question: \*What if falling has a direction, and aligning with it reduces energy cost?\*



\---



\## 3. Anomalies That Cf Could Explain



| Phenomenon | Current explanation | Cf hypothesis |

|------------|---------------------|----------------|

| Condor flaps only 1% of flight time | Extreme thermal efficiency | Cf → 1 reduces effective gravity |

| Albatross flies in 2 m/s wind | Wave updrafts supplement dynamic soaring | Cf → 1 allows glide in still air |

| Great snipe flies 8,700 m at night | Powered flight, not soaring | Could Cf be higher at altitude? |

| Transoceanic migration without refueling | Fat reserves + efficient aerodynamics | Cf reduces energy expenditure below theoretical minimum |



These are not contradictions. They are \*\*invitations to measure Cf\*\*.



\---



\## 4. How to Measure Cf



| Method | What it measures | Feasibility |

|--------|------------------|-------------|

| Synchronized gravimeter array | Micro-variations in g beneath soaring birds | High (fundable) |

| Atomic clock network | Time dilation differences between wings (if Cf affects time) | Medium |

| Accelerometer + GPS + wind sensor | Subtract known forces to reveal residual | High |



A dedicated Cf sensor would need:



\- Three-axis accelerometer (precision: 1e-6 g)

\- Atomic clock (or GPS-disciplined oscillator)

\- Wind and thermal sensors (to subtract atmospheric effects)

\- Data logging at >100 Hz



\---



\## 5. Falsification



The Cf hypothesis is falsifiable:



\- If no gravitational anomaly is detected beneath soaring birds, Cf is negligible at bird scale.

\- If Cf is constant (always 1 or always 0), it is not a variable.

\- If Cf correlates perfectly with thermal activity, it is redundant.



\*\*Null result:\*\* Cf measurement gap remains unfilled; current models are sufficient.



\---



\## 6. Conclusion



Cf is an unmeasured variable in flight dynamics. Its absence does not prove its non-existence. This document calls for the development of instruments to measure coherence with the ambient gravity gradient — in birds, aircraft, and spacecraft.



The gap is not a failure. It is a research opportunity.
