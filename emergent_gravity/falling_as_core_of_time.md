\## Abstract



Standard timekeeping is based on periodic motion: Earth's rotation (day), Earth's orbit (year), atomic vibrations (second). This document proposes that these are not time itself but \*\*echoes\*\* of a more fundamental process: falling. Time is not a dimension. Time is the rate of fall.

| Clock | Basis | Scale |

|-------|-------|-------|

| Day | Earth's rotation | Local (planetary) |

| Year | Earth's orbit | Local (solar) |

| Atomic second | Cesium vibration | Local (quantum) |



Each is a measure of \*\*orbital or oscillatory motion\*\* — not of falling.



What we call "time" is the amplified rhythm of one level of the cosmic cascade: Earth falling around the Sun. But the real time is the fall itself.

\## 3. Falling as the Clock



If falling creates time, then:



\- \*\*A stationary object (if one could exist) would be timeless.\*\* But nothing is stationary.

\- \*\*The rate of falling determines the rate of time.\*\* A faster fall does not mean faster time; it means a different reference frame.

\- \*\*Coherent falling may synchronize time.\*\* Particles falling together share a common temporal frame.



This is not speculation. It is a restatement of gravitational time dilation in general relativity: clocks run slower in deeper gravitational wells (faster falling toward the center).

We do not have a clock for radial fall because we are falling with it. A fish cannot measure the water it swims in.

\## 5. Measuring the Fall



We cannot measure the primordial fall directly. But we can measure \*\*differences in falling\*\* — which we already call time dilation.



| Comparison | What it measures |

|------------|------------------|

| Clock on Earth vs. clock in orbit | Difference in falling depth (gravity) |

| Clock on Earth vs. clock on fast rocket | Difference in falling speed (velocity time dilation) |

| Clock near black hole vs. clock far away | Extreme difference in falling depth |



These are not corrections to absolute time. They are measurements of \*\*different falling rates\*\*.

\## 6. The Unit of Falling Time



Define \*\*1 fall unit (F)\*\* as the time it takes to fall from rest over a defined distance under constant gravitational acceleration — or the orbital period around a center of mass.



But this is not a single number. It is a \*\*function of the cascade level\*\*:



| Level | Approximate "Fall Year" |

|-------|-------------------------|

| Earth falling around Sun | 1 Earth year (365 days) |

| Sun falling around galactic center | \~230 million Earth years |

| Milky Way falling toward Great Attractor | \~12.5 trillion Earth years |



The primordial fall (Level 6) is unmeasurable because we cannot stand outside it.
